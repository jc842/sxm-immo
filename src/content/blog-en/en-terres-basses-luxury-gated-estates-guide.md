# Terres Basses Luxury Estates Guide: Low-Density Villas & Private Shorelines

Situated on the western tip of French Saint-Martin, Terres Basses (Lowlands) is a private, guarded residential community spanning over 2.5 square miles of pristine coastline.

## Architectural and Zoning Safeguards

- **2.5-Acre Minimum Lot Size**: Strict covenants mandate that every estate sits on a minimum parcel of approximately 10,000 m² (2.5 acres), guaranteeing total acoustic and visual seclusion.
- **Private Access to World-Class Beaches**: Direct access to Baie Longue, Baie aux Prunes (Plum Bay), and Baie Rouge.
- **24/7 Monitored Gatehouses**: Controlled checkpoint access and private security patrols ensure top-tier peace of mind.

Terres Basses estates command the highest valuation and seasonal vacation rental income in Saint-Martin.
