# Foreign Investor Guide: Purchasing Property in Sint Maarten & Saint-Martin

Neither the French nor the Dutch administration imposes restrictions on foreign individuals or corporate entities owning freehold real estate in SXM.

## Due Diligence Steps for Overseas Buyers

1. **Title Verification**: Reviewing land registry records (*cadastre*) for undivided co-ownership (*indivision*) or boundary disputes.
2. **Hurricane Building Grade**: Verifying poured reinforced concrete construction and impact-rated hurricane storm shutters.
3. **Rental License**: Registering holiday rental villas to maximize peak winter season occupancy rates.

Freehold title (*pleine propriété*) provides absolute security of ownership for international investors.
